Who we are

A foundation built around human dignity.

Mugerwa Hope Foundation is being developed in Uganda to create practical and compassionate support for vulnerable people and communities.

  • Respect and dignity for every person
  • Support based on real community needs
  • Sustainable partnerships and opportunities
  • Transparency and responsible use of resources

Our vision

A society where vulnerable people are treated with dignity and have access to support, opportunity and hope.

Our mission

To mobilize people and resources to support vulnerable communities through humanitarian assistance, education, health, inclusion and livelihood initiatives.

Our programs

Where we want to make a difference

Our programs will address immediate needs while helping people and communities become more resilient.
